Segment Drill — segment-by-segment viewer

Each of the 8 segments is a wedge centred on one aṣṭadalapadma (Circuit 3, 8-petal lotus) petal. The drawn wedge — the shaded pie-slice and its two boundary rays — keeps the petal's true edge, ±22.5°, the same geometry as the petal shape itself. The candidate list is deliberately wider than the drawn wedge: a deity qualifies at ±30° from the centre angle, confirmed exact against Chris's own eyesight-check of S1. That extra 7.5° beyond the drawn line is what pulls in the immediately-neighbouring C4/C5 triangle on each side — its shape physically straddles the boundary even though the wedge line doesn't move — and it's why the same deity can legitimately appear in two adjacent segments' lists (e.g. gandhākarṣiṇī in both S1 and S5). The Bindu (śrī śrī mahābhaṭṭārikē) is always included last, in every segment, since every ray starts there. Segments are ordered S1→S8 by the 3rd āvaraṇa's own chant sequence (anaṅgakusumē, anaṅgamēkhalē, anaṅgamadanē, anaṅgamadanāturē, anaṅgarēkhē, anaṅgavēginī, anaṅgāṅkuśē, anaṅgamālinī), not physical clockwise position. Within each segment, deities are listed outside → inside (bhūpura first, Bindu last). S1 is confirmed; S2–S8 await your check.
