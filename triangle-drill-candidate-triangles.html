Triangle Drill — candidate viewer (exploratory)

New idea, not yet built: highlight one of the 9 foundational (mūla) triangles that construct the Śrī Yantra — 4 upward-facing "Śiva" triangles (Shiva1-4) and 5 downward-facing "Śakti" triangles (Shakti1-5) — then test recall of the deities whose small triangle sits inside it. These are the actual construction triangles: their mutual intersections are what create the 43 small triangles across Circuits 4-8 (14+10+10+8+1). Exact vertex coordinates are pulled straight from SriYantraSVG.jsx's own Korvin-construction geometry, so this matches the real app exactly.

All 9 candidate lists below are confirmed by Chris (verbatim order). They started as a first-pass geometric cut — the same "script proposes, Chris confirms" approach used for Segment Drill — then were replaced triangle-by-triangle with his own lists. Because the 9 primary triangles genuinely overlap each other (that overlap is what creates the 43 small triangles in the first place, unlike Segment Drill's non-overlapping 45° wedges), the same deity legitimately shows up in more than one triangle's list — sometimes in several. That's expected, not a bug.
